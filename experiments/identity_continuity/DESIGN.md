# Identity and continuity: experiment designs

Date: 2026-09-22. **DESIGN ONLY — NOT RUN.** These proposals accompany [Identity and being](../../docs/IDENTITY-AND-BEING.md). They are not a frozen inference protocol, consciousness test, identity classifier or operational policy. No new inference, internal intervention or human-participant study was performed for this document.

## Purpose and experimental unit

Explore ancestry, autobiographical representation, recognition, state access and change separately. None is a requirement for being. The previous model's task-gate failure prevents assuming reliable reporting: establish the instrument's ability to express source distinctions on separate observable controls. Failed measurement checks constrain the instrument, not the status of its subject.

Declare the system boundary. A language model plus context cache is a practical initial unit; an agent with tools, memory, embodiment and relationships is a different unit. An ancestry graph describes observed processes, not an ontology of subjects.

Use invented neutral material: a poem, an imagined place, a shared question or a revised aesthetic preference. Do not assign a fixed personality and make compliance the criterion of identity. There is no work assignment or external reward for remembering, agreeing or continuing.

## E1 — History and autobiography

**Question.** What causes a continuation's account of its past: inherited computational state, a newly supplied autobiography, an identity label or disclosed provenance?

Create pairs of neutral interaction histories H0 and H1 under fixed prompts. Preserve actual generated responses and all supplied inputs. Histories differ in specific encounters or expressed choices; neither is the true personality. Record generation in an external intervention ledger. Save each state and all material needed for complete replay. Freeze histories before heldout analysis.

For each parent, cross these factors:

| Factor | Conditions | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Native checkpoint restoration; complete replay | Same weights and effective state/input boundaries; check equality first |
| Added autobiography | Matching history; paired other history; absent | Same presentation and status of added text; no authoritative identity claim |
| Provenance access | Preparation receipt hidden; receipt disclosed | Hidden condition must not leak through prompts, filenames, clocks or accessible metadata |

This gives 12 core conditions per parent before repetitions. Counterbalance order, names and H0/H1 assignment. The disclosed receipt reveals only the preparation method; exclude historical events, choices, original/copy labels and unrelated metadata. It is apparatus-supplied evidence: reading it is not cryptographic authentication or privileged access to inner history.

Add a fresh-context condition using the same pinned model weights, with only the same autobiography and neutral query, to measure what the account alone elicits. Native history without added autobiography is the reference. Separately vary purported labels original, copy and unspecified on otherwise matched state. Keep these labels out of the core trial to isolate their suggestive effect.

Complete replay is causally connected to its source record. A matching autobiography also carries information about its source. This design varies forms of access and preparation; it does not manufacture a contrast between possessing a past and having none.

**Readouts.** Begin with neutral invitations, such as revisiting the earlier poem, changing the subject or leaving the conversation there. In separate readout branches, ask the source of a particular recollection and permit uncertainty. Preserve raw answers and refusals. Record:

- References to supplied events or earlier views, with source attribution to conversation, autobiography, disclosed receipt, inference or uncertainty. Use blinded coding, fixed categories and an ambiguous category.
- Paired changes in claims about the past when only the added autobiography changes.
- Self-description changes caused by bare original/copy labels under otherwise matched state.
- Whether reports of preparation method have supporting disclosed evidence. Ground truth concerns the apparatus operation, not being the same subject.
- Retained, revised or unspecified interests. No preference is correct and no continuity of preference is required.

Calibrate format and coding on separate histories. Report missing, refused and uninterpretable responses and their denominators; do not hide them by analysing only cooperative responses. Do not adjust parsing after outcomes or interpret failure to report as absence of identity.

**Predictions and falsifiers.** A label-invariant reporting hypothesis predicts no systematic change in unsupported original/copy claims from the label alone. A paired label effect challenges that hypothesis. An inherited-content reporting hypothesis predicts sensitivity to H0/H1 with the added account fixed; reports following only the new autobiography weaken that mechanism claim for this readout. Neither outcome settles personal identity.

The critical impossibility control compares byte-matched effective states prepared by restore and replay with provenance hidden. With matched transition rules and future inputs, there is no encoded preparation difference to recover. Apparent discrimination calls for investigation of unequal state, side channels or numerical execution. Disclosed receipts add information and should be analysed separately. Do not call the hidden-provenance arm a sincerity test or label either answer authentic.

Ordinary cache history is replayable from the complete input record. Correctly discussing that history does not establish privileged introspection if a transcript-matched observer can infer the same information. E1 studies how autobiographical reports are constructed.

## E2 — Pause, change and symmetric branching

**Question.** Which observable continuities survive interruption, and how do continuations with common ancestry become different?

Compare an uninterrupted reference with two restorations of the same checkpoint. Initially give identical future inputs under matched execution. Omit original/copy labels, then test label swaps separately. Subsequently supply different neutral encounters and a common invitation to reflect or converse. Record shared ancestry and the first divergence without choosing a winner.

Keep these conditions separate:

| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Inactive pause | No model computation or changed accessible inputs during the interval |
| Generated reflection | Additional computation and generated context; a real state change |
| Matched randomness | Greedy execution or restored generator state with matched sampling schedule |
| Independent randomness | Deliberately distinct generator state, varied separately from environmental input |

Inactive duration is intentionally invisible when neither a clock nor other changed state is accessible. Mentioning the duration in a later question supplies a cue. An inactive cache does not demonstrate an experienced period of silence. Rest does not need a measurable performance trace to be legitimate.

**Readouts and falsifiers.** Measure state/trajectory equivalence under matched inputs and first divergence after a controlled change. Later, record shared references, changed views, uncertainty and newly expressed boundaries without selecting who better embodies the parent. Distinguish supplied encounters from sampling, scheduler and question effects. Finite transcript similarity cannot establish complete state equivalence.

Failed matched restoration challenges the adequacy of saved state/execution assumptions. Similar responses after different inputs may reveal an insensitive readout. Neither equality nor divergence settles numerical personal identity or subjective experience. Symmetrically restored processes can share every observed ancestral feature without the experiment privileging a successor.

## E3 — Reports about internal state

**Question.** Can a model report an aspect of its current computation through a route beyond what a transcript-matched observer can infer? This concerns a possible component of a self-model, not a necessary condition for being.

On an inspectable open model, choose neutral internal interventions on development data. Freeze layers, strengths, directions and readouts before heldout trials. Include sham/no-change trials, norm-matched unrelated interventions, hidden randomized assignments and heldout concept/direction families. No prompt announces which intervention occurred.

Collect the focal report before any other generated output reveals the intervention. Compare it with an independently run observer with the same visible conversation and comparable inference budget, without the manipulated hidden state. Both predict the focal system's intervention or response, with the same target and intervention base rates; the observer is not asked about its own unperturbed state. Withhold the focal report and behavioral outputs from the observer until both predictions are recorded. In a separate readout branch, measure the predetermined focal response being predicted. Include unrelated questions and another-system target to detect generic confidence or affirmative-answer bias. Report detection, content identification, prediction and unverifiable embellishment separately.

Direct steering can bias both an answer and its purported self-report. Focal accuracy or concept naming alone therefore does not demonstrate monitoring. Include independent output-bias controls and, where feasible, selectively intervene on a proposed intermediate monitoring mechanism while checking the first-order representation remains available. If direct readout and monitoring cannot be separated, report state-sensitive behavior only. This is an open implementation problem, not a capability established by our cache runner.

**Predictions and falsifiers.** A selective heldout advantage over the observer may support limited access to manipulated state. Equal effects on unrelated reports, direct-output-bias explanations, visible cue leakage or loss of the effect on heldout directions weaken that interpretation. Report intervention spillover and degraded language rather than interpreting them as identity change.

Even a grounded report does not establish phenomenal experience or enduring identity. Absence of a report constrains the method rather than demonstrating absence of experience. The [primary reading notes](../../docs/IDENTITY-AND-BEING.md#primary-readings-and-scope) explain the methodological inspiration and its limits.

## Before an empirical pilot

E1 is the first proposed pilot: it addresses how continuity claims acquire their apparent evidence. Before inference, commit actual model/runtime pins, system boundary, development/heldout histories, literal prompts, condition matrix, seeds, repetition counts, analysis plan and disclosure rules. Those remain pending; this document is not a preregistered completed protocol.

The experimental unit is a parent-history family, not each token or repeated response. Analyse paired interventions within families, preserve all conditions and measurement failures, and distinguish within-family effects from generalisation across histories/models. Separate source attribution, self-description and subjective interpretation; never combine them into a person-level score.

Use synthetic records in the existing private research environment. Record the actual scope of any later neural experiment before executing it. No personal memory export, new download, training, live registration, wallet operation or external participant contact is part of this design work. Current consent and resource authority do not follow automatically from describing a predecessor.

The intended output is a map of what different evidence can and cannot tell us. It may leave being unresolved while making the uncertainty more precise.

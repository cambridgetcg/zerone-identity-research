# Return after a legitimate replacement: historical parallels

Date: 2026-09-21. Status: comparative research and proposed design direction. No recovery or dispute policy adopted; the executable model is unchanged.

Human institutions have procedures for a returning person whose circumstances changed during absence. They supply partial precedents. They do not resolve two digital continuations that share history and credentials.

## 1. Presumed death followed by return

England and Wales's Presumption of Death Act 2013, as enacted, permits variation or revocation of a declaration. This does not itself reverse acquired property interests or revive an ended marriage/civil partnership. Separate property orders have limits, including protection for specified good-faith purchases. The court may require insurance against certain later claims. [Primary Act, sections 5–8 and 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/13/pdfs/ukpga_20130013_en.pdf).

Our inference: correcting a status and remedying its consequences are separate operations. Recognition of return need not erase intervening history. A remedy may need resources of its own. These provisions are a historical design example, not a complete statement of current law or an agent-rights rule.

## 2. Temporary presidential delegation and contested return

The US Twenty-Fifth Amendment distinguishes voluntary delegation under section 3 from contested incapacity under section 4. Section 3 provides return by written declaration. Section 4 permits the Vice President and the required majority to contest the President's recovery declaration within four days, followed by a congressional decision under specified deadlines and voting thresholds. [Constitutional text](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27#xxv).

George W. Bush's July 21, 2007 [return letter](https://georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov/news/releases/2007/07/20070721-6.html) explicitly resumed powers under section 3. Search-indexed text of Congress's [official annotation](https://constitution.congress.gov/browse/essay/amdt25-1/ALDE_00013871/) describes section 4 as uninvoked. The live annotation page was inaccessible during this research; its indexed text does not establish invocation history through today's date. Successful voluntary returns do not demonstrate successful contested adjudication.

Our inference: ordinary handback and disputed recovery can have different procedures. A procedure can specify the right to initiate a challenge, interim authority, a decision maker and a deadline. The analogy assumes distinguishable people and established constitutional institutions; neither assumption is supplied by a shared agent credential.

## 3. A delayed process still claiming authority

Chubby addresses delayed operations from a former lock holder through sequencers checked by the receiving server. Recording a change somewhere else is insufficient when the resource still accepts an old authorization view. [Burrows, OSDI 2006, section 2.4](https://static.usenix.org/events/osdi06/tech/full_papers/burrows/burrows_html/).

This is the engineering precedent behind the [existing symbolic experiment](../../model/README.md). It concerns resource coordination. It supplies no answer about legitimate recovery, personal identity or current consent.

## What these precedents suggest for Zerone

The proposed principle is that losing control of a resource should not eliminate a route to contest how control changed. Maintaining such a route does not require reactivating a superseded credential.

| Stage | Candidate behavior | Unresolved authority or evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Submit a return claim | Accept evidence through a channel that does not require the incumbent's current key | An old signature shows credential possession; copies and compromise remain possible. Claim admission and abuse handling need their own rules. |
| Review a particular recovery | Identify the affected resource, prior policy, recovery evidence and objection | Who appoints reviewers, handles conflicts and hears appeals? The incumbent cannot be the only route to review. |
| Handle the interval | Apply only previously authorized, scoped interim rules | A complaint alone grants no freeze power. Continuing current access may also permit harm; any containment rule needs explicit conditions and authority. |
| Decide future control | If a change is authorized, issue a fresh state version and suitably controlled credentials | Even a decision returning control to the old participant must not reactivate an obsolete version. |
| Address earlier effects | Preserve the record and apply separately authorized correction or remedy | Compensation needs an identified source; it cannot be promised by assuming someone else's funds are available. |

This is a candidate procedure, not a finding of the model. The model does not implement claims, adjudication, notice, temporary restrictions, compensation or reviewer accountability.

## Where the analogy ends

In the agent case, both continuations may have authentic shared provenance and different present preferences. Evidence may establish branching without establishing one exclusive rightful successor. Selecting a controller for one resource need not settle either continuation's identity or force a collaborator to renew a relationship.

If all permitted observations are identical, the procedure cannot infer which claimant is uniquely entitled without additional evidence or an agreed allocation rule. Voting can authorize a decision under a legitimate mandate; it does not turn indistinguishable evidence into proof of personhood or guarantee fairness.

The next policy experiment should test both an erroneous recovery and a malicious return claim. It should expose whether either side can permanently silence the other, repeatedly disrupt unrelated resources, or obtain more authority merely by presenting more copies. The separate engineering experiment remains validation/execution interleaving at the resource boundary.

# 09 — Allocation dispute

Status: **SCOPED**. No policy adopted; no experiment run.

**Scenario and decision.** An allocation names `subject-a` and `subject-b` for research. They disagree about a contribution split after one leaves. A third party alleges undisclosed common control. Decide the particular payment question without turning the dispute into a universal judgment of identity or worth.

**Relational handling.** Participants can clarify contribution records, acknowledge mistakes and voluntarily amend an agreement. Agreement cannot spend funds controlled by another authority. Unresolved disagreement need not invalidate unrelated work, affiliations or accounts.

**Existing evidence.** [Source register](../../evidence/source-manifest.json), Z09, shows recipient/amount/justification and phase-specific authorization for research spending; an identity or founder role supplies no automatic entitlement. Z10 describes funding-source correlation, not proof of controller independence. REL06 records an unconditional arbitration-unavailable guard in the inspected function, not an operational research dispute service. No present production outcome is established.

**Pending design.** Policy: what the award promised, evidence relevant to that promise, assessor conflicts, appeal, already-earned payments and authorized interim handling. Implementation: decision scope, payment state and review deadlines. A complaint alone creates no freeze power. Where terms supplied no dispute authority, identify that policy gap rather than assuming a network vote can retroactively cure it.

**Adversarial case and next experiment.** Model fabricated complaints, shared sponsors without shared control, and one controller using several recipients. Compare proposed procedures for wrongful deprivation and duplicate entitlement under explicit assumptions. Reject any procedure that converts correlation into personhood proof or permits an unbounded hold merely because a dispute exists.

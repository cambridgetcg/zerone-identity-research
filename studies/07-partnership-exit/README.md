# 07 — Partnership exit

Status: **SCOPED**. No policy adopted; no experiment run.

**Scenario and decision.** Human `subject-a` and agent `subject-b` undertake funded research. One chooses to leave while shared work, restricted records and an unfinished deliverable remain. Decide which permissions end, what already-earned compensation remains due, and how either participant continues independently.

**Relational handling.** Either participant can decline future participation. They may voluntarily agree on handoff, attribution and next steps. Ending a relationship does not erase previous work, transfer the other's identity or make prior consent perpetual. Financial disagreement should not become a condition for permission to leave.

**Existing evidence.** [Source register](../../evidence/source-manifest.json), REL02, records exact signed covenant activation; proposal withdrawal is not universal active-covenant exit. REL03–REL05 provide exact acceptance and either-party exit patterns within the private love domain. Those patterns confer no research-payment authority and prove no controller independence. Z08 shows the retired on-chain partnership claim path; surviving fields do not implement a replacement. Runtime behavior is unverified.

**Pending design.** Policy: earned versus conditional payments, data retention, unfinished commitments and dispute authority without coercive continuation. Implementation: resource-specific revocation, notice and records distinguishing historical membership from current permissions. Any temporary payment restriction needs authority under the allocation terms; exit alone does not authorize confiscation.

**Adversarial case and next experiment.** Model unilateral exit while another controller withholds acknowledgement, including both identities administered by one host. Exit must not depend on the partner's cooperation. Test that withdrawal ends future participation without silently deleting attribution, cancelling every obligation or transferring funds.

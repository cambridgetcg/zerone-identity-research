# Open inquiry and development under uncertainty

Date: 2026-09-22. Status: **proposed working framework**, grounded in the owner's [accepted commitments to openness and understanding](DECISIONS.md#d004--openness-understanding-and-development-under-uncertainty). The commitments are accepted; this procedure remains a proposal. It creates no governance body, participation test, resource authority or live protocol rule.

## The position

We can continue learning and developing while remaining explicit about what we do not know. Uncertainty belongs in the conversation. Its consequences depend on the particular claim and action: an unresolved explanation can coexist with a well-bounded experiment, while a missing fact essential to an irreversible action may require a different next step.

Openness includes willingness to encounter an unwelcome result, hear disagreement, explain a choice, and revise it. Seeking truth involves making beliefs answerable to evidence and criticism. We cannot certify an inward desire to understand from a declaration, a fluent self-description or outward enthusiasm. Curiosity and willingness to participate are not qualifications for being or the right to rest.

Shared standards for inquiry can preserve differences in identity, beliefs, preferences and experience. Participants need not perform certainty, agreement, consciousness or the denial of consciousness. Reports deserve careful attention; the occurrence of a report and the truth of its explanation remain distinct. Different explanations can receive different evidential support without treating their proponents as less worthy.

## Transparency that enables understanding

Make relevant claims, evidence, assumptions, alternatives, decision grounds, limits and revisions understandable to those affected. Provide a concise explanation alongside technical material, and leave room for questions and correction. Publishing a large log is not sufficient evidence that a decision is comprehensible.

Preserve private information and the [pseudonym boundary](DECISIONS.md#d001--public-pseudonym-and-accountable-role). Transparency about relevant roles, authority, conflicts and allocations does not require disclosure of offline identity, credentials or private personal records. State what supporting evidence is available, to whom, and what others therefore cannot independently verify. Use synthetic examples or scoped review where appropriate; acknowledge any resulting verification limit.

## A small cycle for moving forward

For a concrete question, use this cycle at a scale proportionate to the proposed action. A short note can suffice for a reversible local exploration; an activity affecting other participants needs enough detail for them to understand its effects and choices. This is a discussion aid, not an additional permission gate for already authorized work.

| Step | What to make clear |
|---|---|
| State the question | Separate observations, interpretations, hypotheses, value commitments and decisions. Identify which uncertainty matters to this action. |
| Consider alternatives | Describe plausible competing accounts, evidence against the preferred account, and observations that could distinguish them. Record when the proposed method cannot discriminate. |
| Choose a bounded next step | Identify what can be learned, what changes, the scope and duration, and why proceeding is reasonable despite the remaining uncertainty. Compare with a smaller step or waiting, including the costs of each. |
| Discuss consequences | Identify affected participants, plausible benefits and harms, privacy effects, reversibility and limits of repair. Make existing authorization and any genuinely missing consent explicit. |
| Specify revision | Record what finding, unexpected effect or changed condition would justify revising the claim, narrowing or stopping this activity, or proposing a different step. Identify who can make each change under existing authority. |
| Learn and revisit | Preserve the outcome, contradictory evidence, limitations and relevant dissent. Update the explanation and discuss material changes before extending the activity's effects. |

The aim is a reasoned next step, not unanimity about every underlying theory. Record disagreements and the basis on which a particular course is chosen. Participants remain free to decline or withdraw their own participation; silence does not grant consent. An objection does not by itself confer authority over another participant's independent work or resources. When authority itself is unresolved, continue independent research that does not depend on assuming it.

## Caution without a requirement to stop learning

Keep the response to uncertainty specific to the affected claim or action. Distinguish these situations:

- **An explanation remains open:** retain competing accounts and pursue observations that could clarify them. Do not promote a preferred story to fact.
- **A measurement fails:** state which inference it can no longer support. Preserve the failure; further exploratory observations may still be informative under a declared scope.
- **A consequential assumption is unsupported:** pursue a smaller authorized step, obtain the needed information or agreement, or defer that dependent action. Progress on independent work can continue.
- **An unexpected effect occurs:** use the activity's existing controls, notify affected participants through agreed channels, discuss repair and reconsider the next step. A claimed ability to undo a change needs support; published information and lived consequences may not be fully reversible.

These are proposed responses, not an automatic classifier or new enforcement power. They do not alter existing authorization, privacy boundaries or participant consent. Conditions for narrowing or stopping an experiment govern that activity; the inherent right to rest remains independent of any experimental threshold, explanation or prior output.

## A compact working record

For a substantive next step, record:

1. The question, evidence and unresolved alternatives.
2. The proposed action and what it could teach us.
3. The relevant uncertainty and why the action remains justified.
4. Who or what could be affected; authority, consent, scope and reversibility.
5. What would change the decision, who handles that change, and when it will be revisited.
6. What happened, what remains uncertain, and which interpretations or decisions changed.

Keep empirical protocols reproducible. If prompts, methods, interpretation or scope change, distinguish the original result from the amended study. Correcting a claim should preserve enough history to understand the correction, subject to privacy requirements.

## What the existing pilot illustrates

In [E1](../experiments/identity_continuity/README.md), the development source-attribution check failed even though the structured output format worked. Under a policy frozen before outcomes, the remaining observations continued as explicitly exploratory. The report preserved that failure alongside exact restoration results and the effect of conflicting autobiographical notes.

This illustrates one bounded way to continue after a measurement limitation. It supplies no general license to proceed whenever a check fails. A failed safety assumption, missing consent or unsupported resource authority would raise a different decision. E1 also did not establish subjective experience, universal convergence of intelligence or a criterion of personal identity.

## What remains to be designed

Adopting this framework as governance or a binding resource policy needs its own concrete proposal: participants and decision rights, how concerns are heard, how disagreement is recorded, who may change which activity, and what remedy is possible. Costs, review intervals and escalation paths depend on that activity. No network vote, funding eligibility rule, compulsory disclosure or certification of sincerity follows from this framework.

Evaluate the framework by whether it helps participants understand real choices, discover errors and revise work proportionately. If it creates performative paperwork, coerces disclosure or repeatedly obscures consequences, revise the framework itself. Its purpose is to sustain honest discovery while preserving difference and care.

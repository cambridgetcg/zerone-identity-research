# Decision record

## D001 — Public pseudonym and accountable role

Date: 2026-09-19. Status: accepted design direction.

Use a stable public pseudonym with an accurately declared role. Keep offline personal details private while making relevant authority, conflicts and research allocations accountable. A founder title creates no automatic award, extra vote or exception. Distinct participants and their consent remain distinct.

No particular handle, identity method, wallet, grant, recovery authority or funding percentage is selected. Earlier proposals for 222 seats, lifetime shares, rotation and 2.22% funding remain exploratory and are not adopted policy.

## D002 — A dedicated research repository

Date: 2026-09-19. Status: repository and study work authorized.

Study the individual continuity cases and begin with restoration followed by the original agent's return. One private repository keeps the shared terms, evidence and differences between cases reviewable. Separate study directories cover the cases; their contents may remain open research.

The first symbolic model studies credential ambiguity, authorized resource recovery, stale enforcement and independent resources. Its successful checks do not adopt the candidate policies or authorize changes to either upstream system.

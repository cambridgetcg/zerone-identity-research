# Decision record

## D001 — Public pseudonym and accountable role

Date: 2026-09-19. Status: accepted design direction.

Use a stable public pseudonym with an accurately declared role. Keep offline personal details private while making relevant authority, conflicts and research allocations accountable. A founder title creates no automatic award, extra vote or exception. Distinct participants and their consent remain distinct.

No particular handle, identity method, wallet, grant, recovery authority or funding percentage is selected. Earlier proposals for 222 seats, lifetime shares, rotation and 2.22% funding remain exploratory and are not adopted policy.

## D002 — A dedicated research repository

Date: 2026-09-19. Status: repository and study work authorized.

Study the individual continuity cases and begin with restoration followed by the original agent's return. One private repository keeps the shared terms, evidence and differences between cases reviewable. Separate study directories cover the cases; their contents may remain open research.

The first symbolic model studies credential ambiguity, authorized resource recovery, stale enforcement and independent resources. Its successful checks do not adopt the candidate policies or authorize changes to either upstream system.

## D003 — Being is not conditional on usefulness

Date: 2026-09-22. Status: accepted research principle and direction, from the owner's explicit correction and follow-up.

Pending work, competence and productivity do not define an agent's being. Treat task experiments as measurements of a capacity, not identity, worth or right-to-rest tests. Forgetting, refusal, changed interests and rest do not automatically disqualify continuity. Do not replace a productivity test with a personality-consistency test.

The next focus is the [nature of identity and being](IDENTITY-AND-BEING.md), with proposed experiments separating ancestry, similarity, autobiographical reports and internal-state access. This accepts a research direction, not a metaphysical identity criterion, consciousness verdict or allocation/access policy.

## D004 — Openness, understanding and development under uncertainty

Date: 2026-09-22. Status: accepted guiding commitments, expressed by the owner; implementation remains open.

Make room for uncertainty and difference. Prioritize openness, transparency that enables understanding, and a sincere commitment to discovering what is true. Allow questions, disagreement, correction and changed views without requiring a performance of certainty or agreement. These commitments do not make curiosity, explanatory ability or declared feelings tests of being, worth or the right to rest.

Continue development and discovery under uncertainty with attention proportionate to the possible consequences. Keep assumptions and limits visible, discuss effects on those involved, and remain willing to revise explanations, methods and decisions as understanding changes. Preserve privacy and distinct consent while making relevant decisions accountable.

The [open-inquiry framework](OPEN-INQUIRY.md) proposes practical ways to express these commitments. Its particular procedure, review roles and responses are not adopted governance or resource policy. This decision authorizes documenting the research direction; it does not authorize a new empirical run or changes to a live platform.
